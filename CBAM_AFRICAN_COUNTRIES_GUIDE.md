### **Covered Products:**

- **Emission Factor:** 0.83 kg CO2/kg cement
- **High-Risk Countries:** Egypt, Algeria, Tunisia
- **Why High Carbon:** Clinker production (1,450°C heat)
- **EU Import Volume:** 5 million tonnes/year from Africa
- **Tariff Estimate:** €50-100 per tonne

#### **2. Steel & Iron** ⚙️
- **Emission Factor:** 1.85 kg CO2/kg steel
- **High-Risk Countries:** South Africa, Egypt, Algeria
- **Why High Carbon:** Blast furnaces, coal-based
- **South Africa Exposure:** R25 billion in exports
- **Tariff Estimate:** €75-150 per tonne

#### **3. Aluminum** 🔩
- **Emission Factor:** 8.5 kg CO2/kg aluminum
- **High-Risk Countries:** South Africa, Mozambique, Cameroon
- **Why High Carbon:** Electrolysis process (energy-intensive)
- **Mozambique Impact:** Mozal smelter (primary exporter)
- **Tariff Estimate:** €200-400 per tonne

#### **4. Fertilizers** 🌾
- **Emission Factor:** 3.5 kg CO2/kg fertilizer
- **High-Risk Countries:** Egypt, Algeria, Tunisia, Nigeria
- **Why High Carbon:** Ammonia synthesis (natural gas)
- **EU Import Volume:** 2 million tonnes/year
- **Tariff Estimate:** €100-200 per tonne

#### **5. Hydrogen** ⚡
- **Emission Factor:** 10.0 kg CO2/kg hydrogen (grey hydrogen)
- **Future Risk:** All countries producing from fossil fuels
- **Green Hydrogen Advantage:** Near-zero emissions
